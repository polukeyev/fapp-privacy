FAQ

The short answers.

Do my SMS messages go through your servers?

No. AutoRelay sends messages directly from your device to the destinations you configure. We operate no intermediate server and never receive or store your message content.

How do I pay, and can I cancel?

Pro is a $4.99/month subscription you start inside the app. You can cancel anytime; you keep Pro access until the end of the current billing period. A 14-day refund is available — see the Refund Policy.

What do I need to run it?

An Android 8.0+ device. No root required. For webhooks you just need a URL (n8n, Make, Zapier, or your own endpoint); for Telegram a bot token; for email your SMTP credentials.

Is my data secure?

All app data — SMS history and destination credentials — is stored locally in a SQLCipher-encrypted database and protected by the Android sandbox. Everything is removed when you uninstall the app.
