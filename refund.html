Refund Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

This Refund Policy applies to purchases of the AutoRelay (the app “SMS Forwarding: AutoRelay”) Pro subscription made through Google Play, operated by Sperar, a trade name of Oleg Polukeev (“Sperar”, “we”, “us”, “our”).

Billing via Google Play

Payments for AutoRelay Pro are billed by Google Play to the payment method on your Google account. Refunds are issued back to your original payment method through Google Play. Google’s own refund policies also apply to your purchase.

14-Day Refund

If you are not satisfied with your AutoRelay Pro purchase, you may request a full refund within 14 days of the original purchase date. No reason is required.

Refunds typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days after approval, depending on your bank or payment provider.

How to Request a Refund

Email us at urbandevapp@gmail.com with:

  • your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is in the receipt email from Google Play), and/or
  • the email address of the Google account used for the purchase.

We will process eligible requests promptly through Google Play. For recent purchases you can also request a refund directly from Google via the Google Play refund page.

Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions to stop future renewals. After cancellation, you keep Pro access until the end of your current paid period. Cancelling does not, by itself, trigger a refund — to request a refund, follow the steps above. Uninstalling the App does not cancel your subscription.

Renewals

Refunds for automatic renewals are considered on a case-by-case basis. If you were charged for a renewal you did not intend, contact us within 14 days of the renewal and we will work with you in good faith.

Contact

For any refund question: urbandevapp@gmail.com
